The Rules
I
Draw a song
A random track is pulled from the 200-song deck. The active player has no choice — you play what fate gives you.
II
Two full verses or you're done
No humming, no skipping to the chorus, no mumbling. You must deliver at least two recognisable verses. The crowd decides.
III
Majority vote
The group votes Pass or Fail after the performance. Majority rules. A tie goes to the singer — they survive.
IV
The condemned's final act
Fail and you're out. The song returns to the deck. The eliminated player chooses who faces the mic next — their final act of power.
V
Last voice standing
No points. No medals. Pure survival. When one singer remains, they are crowned the Karaoke Royale champion.
